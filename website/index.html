Relight Studio AI

Change the lighting of a photo after it was taken. An AI model estimates the depth of the scene, and you place and drag lights directly on the image. Surfaces that face a light brighten, the rest darken, and objects cast shadows on what is behind them.

  • Lights you drag on the photo
  • Subject and background separation
  • Light patterns
  • Background replacement
  • Ten presets
  • Runs offline
Download for Windows See the source

The setup is about 2 MB and downloads the rest: about 3 GB with an NVIDIA GPU, or 0.5 GB to run on the CPU. It's a beta: lighting from a single photo is an approximation. See the known limitations.
