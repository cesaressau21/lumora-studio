Relight Studio AI

Public beta 0.1.0 Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit Free and open source

Change the lighting of a photo after it was taken. An AI model estimates the depth of the scene, and you place and drag lights directly on the image. Surfaces that face a light brighten, the rest darken, and objects cast shadows on what is behind them.

How it works

  1. Open a photo, or drop it on the window.
  2. The app estimates its depth and the direction each surface faces.
  3. Place lights and drag them. The photo updates as you move them.
  4. Export a PNG, JPEG or WebP at full resolution.
Download for Windows Read the release notes

The setup is about 2 MB. It then downloads what the app needs: about 3 GB with an NVIDIA GPU, or 0.5 GB to run on the CPU. Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown because the setup isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

It's a beta. Lighting from a single photo is an approximation, not a 3D reconstruction. See the known limitations.

Lights on the photo
Point, area, directional, ambient and rim lights, with depth, color, temperature, softness and falloff.
Subject and background
AI matting separates the subject down to the hair. Each light can affect either one, or both.
Light patterns
Blinds, window panes, leaves, dots or your own image, projected over the scene.
New backgrounds
Put the subject on another photo and get lights that match its direction and color.
Ten presets
From Studio Portrait and Golden Hour to Moonlight and Venetian Blinds.
Runs locally
After the first download it works offline, and no photo leaves your computer.
